Security groups

Every VM port denies inbound and outbound traffic unless a group allows it. Groups can allow traffic from other groups, so rules follow VMs as they move.

Snapshot schedules

Locked snapshots can't be deleted through Katam before their lock ends, and their VMs can't be deleted either. Vault copies go to S3 storage with Object Lock: nobody can delete them before their retention date.
